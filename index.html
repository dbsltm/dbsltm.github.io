I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Interdisciplinary Program in Artificial Intelligence at Seoul National University. My research focuses on trustworthy artificial intelligence and human–AI interaction, with an emphasis on connecting human-centered evaluation to model training and alignment. I study how AI systems reason about social situations, how people evaluate and rely on AI explanations, and how human–AI collaboration can improve model auditing. Through human behavioral studies, model evaluation, and post-training, I aim to develop AI systems that are not only capable but also trustworthy and responsive to human values.

I am advised by Professor Gunhee Kim at SNU Vision & Learning Lab. At CMU, I am visiting Professor Maarten Sap's lab at LTI and work closely with Professor Motahhare Eslami at HCII.

Research

All papers →

How can we design multimodal AI systems whose reasoning is transparent, trustworthy, and aligned with human values, and how do humans perceive, interact with, and sometimes misplace trust in these systems?

  1. Model Understanding

    How can models reliably perceive and interpret socially situated information? I study three levels of reliability: grounding what is present, interpreting what is happening, and judging what is appropriate.

    HalLoc (CVPR 2025) CoCoT (COLM 2026) MM-SCALE (NeurIPS 2026)

  2. Human Evaluation of AI Reasoning

    How do people's beliefs, values, and expectations shape their scrutiny of and reliance on AI reasoning? And how should alignment be measured so that it reflects both users' stated and unstated intent?

    Critical or Compliant? (EMNLP 2026) Right Advice, Wrong Reasons (Preprint)

  3. Toward Safe and Reliable Human–AI Interaction

    How should AI systems be designed to support safe, reliable, and helpful human judgments and interactions? This includes evaluating the downstream effects of human–AI interaction in personal, everyday settings, and building models that can anticipate and cope with the inconsistencies of human behavior across contexts.

    HAAC (Preprint)
