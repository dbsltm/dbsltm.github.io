Eunkyu (Eunice) Park

박은규

Ph.D. Candidate in Artificial Intelligence at Seoul National University (SNUVL), advised by Gunhee Kim. Currently a Visiting Researcher at CMU HCII with Motahhare Eslami, and collaborating closely with Maarten Sap at CMU LTI.

My research centers on the trustworthiness and interpretability of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) — spanning bias-driven hallucinations, multimodal moral reasoning, and scaffolding intermediate reasoning to evaluate and calibrate user trust. I design benchmarks, annotation pipelines, and preference learning techniques to improve transparency, safety, and alignment of multimodal systems.

In my free time I enjoy golf ⛳, football ⚽🩵, and good food and wine with good company ☀️.

  • HalLoc: Token-level Localization of Hallucinations for Vision Language Models
    Eunkyu Park*, Minyeong Kim*, Gunhee Kim
    CVPR 2025
    Abstract
    Hallucinations pose a significant challenge to the reliability of large vision-language models. We propose HalLoc, a dataset for efficient, probabilistic hallucination detection featuring 150K token-level annotated samples across VQA, instruction-following, and image captioning tasks. We also introduce a baseline model offering low-overhead, concurrent hallucination detection during generation.
    Dataset
  • Cognitive Chain-of-Thought: Structured Multimodal Reasoning about Social Situations
    Eunkyu Park et al.
    Pre-print, Under Review · 2025
    Abstract
    We introduce Cognitive Chain-of-Thought (CoCoT), a prompting strategy that scaffolds VLM reasoning through three stages: perception, situation, and norm. CoCoT consistently outperforms CoT and direct prompting (+8% on average) across multiple multimodal benchmarks.
