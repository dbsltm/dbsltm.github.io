Eunkyu (Eunice) Park

박은규 · Ph.D. Candidate, Seoul National University · Visiting Researcher, CMU HCII

I work at the intersection of NLP, computer vision, and HCI, studying how multimodal AI systems reason and how people come to trust them. My research spans three threads: evaluating the trustworthiness of vision-language models (faithfulness of reasoning, localizing perception-to-understanding failures); studying how people perceive, rely on, and are misled by AI explanations; and building benchmarks and alignment methods for multimodal moral and social reasoning. I design human-subject studies to evaluate how users perceive and calibrate trust in these systems, and translate those findings into better learning signals for model alignment.

Advised by Gunhee Kim at SNU Vision & Learning Lab. At CMU HCII I work with Motahhare Eslami and closely with Maarten Sap (LTI).

Research

How can we design multimodal AI systems whose reasoning is transparent, trustworthy, and aligned with human values, and how do humans perceive, interact with, and sometimes misplace trust in these systems?

  1. Trustworthiness & Interpretability of VLMs

    Detecting and localizing failures in vision-language models, examining how model biases lead to unfaithful outputs, and whether model reasoning faithfully reflects human values rather than mimicking them.

    HalLoc (CVPR 2025)

  2. Human Perception of AI Reasoning

    How people evaluate, trust, and are misled by AI reasoning chains. When chain-of-thought explanations support critical thinking versus create false confidence, and what interaction designs encourage careful oversight.

    Critical or Compliant? (EMNLP 2026) CoCoT (COLM 2026)

  3. Multimodal Moral & Social Reasoning

    Benchmarks and preference learning frameworks that capture the continuous, pluralistic nature of human moral judgment across text and image, moving beyond binary labels toward human-aligned supervision.

    MM-SCALE (arXiv 2026) Jury Duty (EMNLP 2026)
